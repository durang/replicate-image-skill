# Prompt Formulas — Copy & Paste Templates

## 📸 IMAGE PROMPTS

### Portrait Photography
```
[Age] [gender] with [hair description], 
[eye color] eyes, [expression], 
[pose: frontal/three-quarter/ profile].
[Clothing description].
Background: [simple studio/outdoor/natural].
Lighting: [type and direction, e.g., Rembrandt from left].
[Style: editorial/beauty/cinematic].
[Technical: 4K, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens].
```

**Example:**
```
A 28-year-old woman with long wavy chestnut hair, 
bright green eyes, gentle smile, three-quarter pose.
Wearing a cream linen blouse.
Simple grey studio background.
Split lighting from left, subtle fill from right.
Editorial beauty photography style.
4K, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens.
```

## 🎬 VIDEO PROMPTS

### Cinematic Scene
```

[Action: what they do, how they do it].
[Environment: specific location + atmosphere].
camera [ONE movement - slow/gentle].
[Lighting: specific type + quality].
[Style: cinematic, film grain, color grade].
[Audio mood: tense/sweet/mysterious].
avoid jitter, avoid bent limbs.
```

**Example:**
```
A weary traveler in a worn leather jacket stands at an
abandoned train platform at night.
Fog rolls in as distant thunder approaches.
Slowly pulls out a faded photograph, studying it intently.
camera gentle push-in from medium to close-up.
Dramatic rim lighting from platform lamp, deep shadows.
Film noir style, teal-orange color grade, subtle grain.
No audio, let ambient sounds of rain build tension.
avoid jitter, avoid bent limbs.
```

### Timeline Storyboard (15 seconds)
```
0-4s: [Wide shot establishing scene].
[Subject] + [environment details].
camera [movement for establishing].
Style: [foundation lighting/atmosphere].

4-9s: [Medium shot - main action].
[Character performs key action].
camera [movement during action].
[Lighting: maintain mood].

9-13s: [Close-up - detail/reaction].
[Hands/face/object - key moment].
camera [push-in/hold on detail].
[Cinematic emphasis on moment].

13-15s: [Final shot - resolve or hook].
[Closing visual/wide reveal/cliffhanger].
camera [movement to final position].
Style: [confirm consistent grading].
avoid jitter, avoid bent limbs, avoid temporal flicker.
```

**Example:**
```
0-4s: Wide shot. A young woman in hiking gear stands at
the edge of a misty mountain viewpoint. Wind whipping
her hair. Camera slow push-in to establish scale.
Misty mountain atmosphere, cool blue tones, fog layers.

4-9s: Medium shot. She pulls out a vintage compass from
her jacket. Studies it with curious expression. Camera
gentle tracking as she turns to look at something off-camera.
Maintain cool atmosphere, natural wind sounds.

9-13s: Close-up on the compass needle spinning wildly.
Then settling pointing toward something beyond the fog.
Camera holds locked-off on compass detail.
Dramatic tension music swells slightly.

13-15s: Wide shot pulling back to reveal she's not alone.
A shadowy figure emerges from the fog behind her.
Camera pulls back slowly.
Same blue tones, final frame holds on her oblivious expression.
avoid jitter, avoid bent limbs, avoid temporal flicker.
```
